Instructions

Go on a mini safari as you search for four different plants: 3 native plants and 1 introduced.

Use the pictures provided and the links on each plant card to learn how to identify the plant.

Think you found your plant? Use the iNaturalist app to post your observation and see if you identified it correctly.
